If you're reading this, chances are you either stumbled upon my portfolio, or you're me — checking in on where I began.

This is my first ever blog post.

And to be honest, I'm writing this with zero expectations. I'm not doing this because I want to become a content creator or because I think I have deep, world-changing thoughts to share.

I'm doing this because I need to.

---

## Why I Started This Blog

I've had a lot of reasons, really.

At first, I thought — maybe I should just document my learnings somewhere. A blog seemed like a neat place to do that. Then I started building my portfolio, and, well… most portfolios have a blog section. Might as well make mine useful.

But truthfully?
I don't have a job right now.
My offer from Turing got revoked. That hit hard.

I'm not in a great place. I'm in a pinch. But I know one thing — I don't want to stay here.

So this blog is my way of moving forward. Even if it's slow. Even if it's just 1% at a time.

---

## What I Hope To Do Here

I'm interested in AI. Machine Learning. Computer Vision.
Am I an expert? Far from it.
But I want to be. And I'm going to learn my way there.

This blog will be:

- A learning diary
- A place to reflect on my mistakes
- A space to break down the projects I build
- And sometimes, just a corner of the internet where I talk to myself

If someday someone reads these and learns something — great.
But if not, that's fine too. These are for me.
To not forget. To track progress. To stay honest with myself.

---

## A Bit of Backstory

Back in my 4th semester, I was told to build a portfolio. While exploring different formats and designs, I kept noticing that people had blog sections.
And I thought — why?

Why does a CS student need a blog? I'm not a journalist. I'm not a news editor.

But as time passed, it clicked.

The blog isn't meant for others.
It's a reflection space.
A progress tracker.
A digital memory.

It's less about broadcasting and more about becoming.

---

## So… What Now?

This is just the beginning.

I'll be dumping my learnings, documenting projects, and maybe even sharing random insights here.
If I think something's good enough — I'll share it.
If not, it stays here, quietly helping me grow.

And who knows — maybe along the way, this blog will help me connect with people who vibe with what I'm building.

---

**I'm just getting started.**
Let's see where this goes.

– Jawahar
